History

Every change of the archive, newest first, and the snapshot that holds it. The pages are redrawn on every run, but a snapshot is taken only when something changed; the newest of every day is kept. To look at an older version, restore its snapshot with the command shown: a snapshot is kept encrypted, not as files you could open in Finder. The copy goes into a folder of its own next to the archive, which stays as it is, and a link to it appears here.
